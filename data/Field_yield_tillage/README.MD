The 30-m yield data is from QDANN-generated yield maps: https://gee-community-catalog.org/projects/qdann/.

The 30-m tillage data is from STaPL-generated tillage maps: https://gee-community-catalog.org/projects/stapl_tillage/.

The field boundary is from USDA's Crop Sequence Boundary (CSB) dataset: https://www.nass.usda.gov/Research_and_Science/Crop-Sequence-Boundaries/index.php

The processed field-level yield and tillage records for the study area (1999–2023) will be made publicly available upon acceptance of this paper and are available from the authors upon reasonable request.
